Pranaav Jain & Associates, established in 2012, is a Chennai-based CA firm built on one conviction — that SMEs and MSMEs deserve the same quality of professional advice that large enterprises access.

The firm is personally led by Pranaav Jain, FCA, CMA, CS — a multi-qualified professional with qualifications spanning Chartered Accountancy, Cost Accounting, Company Secretary and AICA Level-2 certification. Every engagement is personally handled with hands-on involvement from start to finish.
